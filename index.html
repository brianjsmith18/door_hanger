WHAT IS A TOWNSHIP BOARD?
THE ELECTED CHECK ON THE TOWNSHIP TRUSTEE

The Plain Township Board...

  • Approves budgets and contracts.
  • Reviews township finances.
  • Sets tax rates and salaries.
  • Public Safety (Fire & EMS).
  • Funds Township Assistance Program.
  • Protects taxpayers through responsible oversight.

THE CURRENT BOARD'S RECORD
WHY INDEPENDENT OVERSIGHT IS NEEDED
  • Lawn care contract to a relative; lawn care costs rose from $15,316 in 2021 to $34,065 in 2025.
  • Township jobs and contracts to spouses, relatives, and/or household members of Township officials.
  • Approved substantial 2026 salary increases: 37% for the Trustee and 50% for Township Board members.
  • Meetings are only publicized 48 hours in advance by a note on the Township office door.
Sources: Indiana Gateway; Plain Township records and Board meeting minutes (7/17/2025).

WHY ELECT NOFTZ, OSBUN, & SMITH?

Rebecca Noftz, Robert Osbun, and Brian Smith are committed to restoring oversight, transparency, and accountability to the Plain Township Board.

We will...

  • Protect fire, EMS, and township assistance funding and vote to roll back Township Board Member salary increases.
  • Use our budget authority to insist on merit based hiring and eliminate conflicts of interest.
  • Safeguard your tax dollars through competitive bidding for contracts.
  • Post meeting notices online, on social media, and in the local press.
  • Actively seek out and listen to the needs of the community.
  • Publish assistance guidelines and simplify requests for help.
